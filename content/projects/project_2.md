From February to August 2021, I volunteered with the Center for Hellenic Studies at Harvard University to prepare digitized Ancient Greek texts for inclusion in the [Open Greek and Latin Project](https://www.opengreekandlatin.org/).

My work involved proofreading and editing OCR-generated text to ensure accuracy, as well as performing zoning on scanned page images to identify and delineate titles, primary texts, critical apparatus, and other textual elements. I also assigned Uniform Resource Names (URNs) to individual passages to facilitate reference and retrieval.

An excerpt of a text I proofread and edited is available in the [Scaife Viewer](https://scaife.perseus.org/reader/urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0086.tlg004.1st1K-grc1:1-5/).
